    FAQ

    Frequently asked questions

    Everything you need to know about using DiagnosTech on campus.

    How do I book my first appointment?

    Sign in with your university credentials, select a department and provider, then choose an available time slot. You'll receive a confirmation email instantly.

    Is DiagnosTech covered by student health insurance?

    Yes. DiagnosTech accepts all university-sponsored health plans. You can verify your coverage during the booking process before confirming your visit.

    Can I cancel or reschedule an appointment?

    Absolutely. Visit your dashboard, select the appointment, and choose reschedule or cancel. We ask for at least 24 hours notice when possible.

    Are telehealth visits available for all services?

    Most general consultations, mental health sessions, and follow-ups are available via telehealth. Some procedures require an in-person visit at the campus health center.

    How is my health data protected?

    DiagnosTech is fully HIPAA-compliant. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and only authorized medical staff can access your records.
